A Palace That Feels Like Home

Just minutes off the highway, Mishtten rises like a secret — arches, carved balconies and courtyards in the heritage style of Rajasthan, yet warm as a family home. Mornings begin with birdsong over the water, afternoons drift by at the pool, and evenings end under a sky full of stars with dinner from our pure-vegetarian kitchens.

From intimate escapes to grand weddings, every corner is built for memories — and every guest is family.

Good to Know

Frequently Asked

Where is Mishtten Club & Resort located?

Near Kota, in Munderi, Semli Patpariya on State Highway 1, Jhalawar district, Rajasthan 326001 — about 11 km of scenic drive off the highway, with the lake and Mukundra Hills around.

Are pets allowed at the resort?

We love animals, but to keep every guest comfortable, pets are not permitted on the premises.

How many room types are available?

Five categories — Premium Room, Executive Suite, Premium Suite, Royal Suite and the Maharaja Club Presidential Suite — each with a mood of its own. See all rooms.

What amenities can I expect?

An open-air swimming pool with rain dance, six-seater Jacuzzi and steam room, children’s play area with an artificial jungle garden, banquet hall, party lawns, back garden, in-room dining, Wi-Fi and much more. View all amenities.

What dining options are available on-site?

Rajsi — pure-vegetarian multi-cuisine fine dining; Aarna — pool-side open-air dining with live barbecue; and Andaz-e-Shahi — the Jacuzzi hall for private dining. Browse the menu.

Is pricing fixed or does it change?

Room and event pricing is dynamic — it depends on date, room type and current offers. Message us on WhatsApp or call +91 72300 66807 for today’s best rate.
