The Name

Sweet in Every Delight

The word Mishtten folds together two things the family holds dear — Mishi, their daughter, and the German word mitten, meaning “sweet”. So the resort was named sweetly, and then built seriously: arches and carved balconies borrowed from the heritage palaces of Rajasthan, gardens that run past the eye can see, and kitchens where everything is pure vegetarian, cooked the way home food should be.

It is a place designed for the two things we love most — rest, and celebration.

What We Believe

Four Rules of the House

  • Guests first, always — every guest is family, and family is looked after first.
  • Purity without compromise — 100% pure-vegetarian kitchens, fresh local produce, Jain preparations on request.
  • Celebrate loudly — from a quiet dinner to a 500-guest wedding, the scale changes but not the care.
  • Respect the land — solar-assisted power and responsible water management across the property.

The Journey

From Kota to the Munderi Fields

The Kota Years

Where hospitality began

The family ran a well-loved hotel in Kota since 2007 — learning, room by room, what makes a guest feel at home.

The Dream

A palace in the fields

Land was found in Munderi — near Kota on the highway, yet worlds away — with open skies, water and the Mukundra Hills on the horizon.

Mishtten Opens

Arches, lawns and a pool

The palace rose with Rajput arches, arched lounges, sixty-six keys, celebration lawns and a pool crowned by a rain-dance rooftop.

Today

Weddings, getaways, celebrations and concerts — people travel across Rajasthan to spend their most golden hours here. Now it’s your turn.
