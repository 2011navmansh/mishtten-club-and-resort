The Two-Day Package

Everything, On The House List

Our two-day wedding package is a complete answer — stay, stages, power, parking and people to run it all.

  • Premium Rooms & Presidential Suites with lake and Mukundra Hill balconies — 4-guests-per-room (family) and 5-guests-per-suite arrangements
  • Kettle with tea-coffee supply plus 6+2 one-litre mineral water bottles in every room, each day
  • 5000 sq.ft. Rajshahi Mahal for lunch, rituals and after-parties; 1-lakh sq.ft. Rani Bagh for musical evenings, reception and dinner
  • Swimming pool with rain-dance rooftop for a full day — morning turmeric ceremony, evening ring ceremony
  • Electricity plus two generators with complete diesel (320 kVA palace, 125 kVA garden)
  • Kitchen and store spaces for wedding food prep, staff quarters for ~120 (caterers, drivers, planners, photographers)
  • Terrace & arched balconies for vows, henna and family rituals; parking with lights for ~500 cars, with guards
  • Luggage team, housekeeping for interiors, cleaners for public areas, 80 high-voltage garden lights and an electrician on call
  • Lake-side water body for beautiful boat entries

Note: We have in-house tent, light & flower — but you are free to book them from outside as well. For more information, call +91 72300 66807 / +91 72300 66805.
